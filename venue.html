Venue, travel & stay

Sharda University, Knowledge Park III, Greater Noida — how to get there, where to stay, and what to see if you extend your visit.

Location

Sharda University, Greater Noida

ICICSC 2027 is hosted on the Sharda University campus in Knowledge Park III, Greater Noida, in the Delhi National Capital Region.

The conference occupies the auditorium and adjoining seminar halls of the Sharda School of Computing Science & Engineering — all within a few minutes' walk, with the exhibition space, dining hall and registration desk alongside.

Sharda Hospital, a multi-speciality teaching hospital, is 200 metres away. Wi-Fi is open to all delegates, and every plenary session is streamed live from the hybrid studio.

Rooms in the university guest house are available to delegates at a subsidised rate, allocated first come, first served. Request one on the registration form.

International delegates

Visa and invitation letters

Foreign delegates normally require a Conference Visa to attend an academic conference in India. Apply well in advance — clearance can take several weeks and the process differs by nationality.

  1. Complete your registration and payment.
  2. Email info@icicsc2027.sharda.ac.in with your paper ID, your name exactly as it appears in your passport, your passport number, its date and place of issue, your date of birth, nationality, and the address of the Indian mission where you will apply.
  3. An official invitation letter on Sharda University letterhead, signed by the General Chair, is issued within ten working days.
  4. Apply at indianvisaonline.gov.in and attend your appointment with the invitation letter and the acceptance email.

An invitation letter states that your paper has been accepted and your registration confirmed. It is not a guarantee of a visa, and the organisers cannot intervene in a consular decision. Please do not book non-refundable travel before your visa is granted.

Before you travel

  • Passport valid for at least six months beyond your date of entry.
  • Travel and medical insurance for the full period of stay.
  • Printed copies of the acceptance email, invitation letter and registration receipt.
  • Check whether your nationality requires additional clearance for a conference visa.
Around the venue

Places to visit nearby

Delegates travelling from abroad often extend their stay. These are within a comfortable day trip of the campus.

The capital

Delhi

The capital: Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Humayun’s Tomb and Purana Qila for Mughal history; Akshardham and the Lotus Temple; India Gate and Rajpath, best seen lit up in the evening.

117 km

Vrindavan

One of the holiest towns for the Vaishnavism tradition, believed to be where Lord Krishna spent his childhood. Many major temples.

125 km

Mathura

Known as the birthplace of Lord Krishna — among the most significant pilgrimage destinations in northern India.

161 km

Kurukshetra

The setting of the Mahabharata, and one of the greatest pilgrimage sites in India.

171 km

Haridwar

One of the seven holiest places in India. The evening Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri is the reason most people come.

189 km

Rishikesh

Gateway to the Garhwal Himalayas, at the confluence of the Ganga and Chandrabhaga. Yoga ashrams and white-water rafting.

Further afield, within a long day's drive: Jhunjhunu (198 km) and Sikar (243 km) in Rajasthan, Pauri Garhwal (203 km) in Uttarakhand, and Manikaran (160 km) in the Parvati Valley, Himachal Pradesh. An optional guided tour is organised on the afternoon of 30 May 2027 for delegates who stay on — indicate your interest on the registration form.

Accessibility

Access and inclusion

The venue is step-free throughout, with lift access to all halls, accessible washrooms on every floor, and reserved seating at the front of each hall.

  • Sign-language interpretation can be arranged with four weeks' notice.
  • A quiet room is available throughout the conference.
  • Dietary requirements are catered for when declared on the registration form.
  • First aid beside the registration desk, with Sharda Hospital 200 metres away.

If there is anything else that would make attending easier, tell us on the registration form or write to access@icicsc2027.sharda.ac.in. We would much rather hear early than have you go without.
