Steps to register

  1. Transfer the fee

    Pay the applicable amount to the account above. International delegates should use the SWIFT code and bear all remitting-bank charges.

  2. Save the proof

    Keep the transaction reference number and a PDF or screenshot of the payment confirmation.

  3. Complete the registration form

    Fill in the online form with your paper ID, category, transaction reference and the payment proof. One form per registration.

  4. Receive confirmation

    A confirmation email with your receipt is issued within five working days. Contact the secretariat if it does not arrive.
