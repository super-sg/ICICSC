About the conference

Shaping the Next Paradigm of Intelligent, Autonomous, and Sustainable Computing

The International Conference on Intelligent, Connected and Sustainable Computing (ICICSC 2027) is a premier two-day international forum hosted by the Sharda School of Computing Science and Engineering, Sharda University, Greater Noida, on 28–29 May 2027 in a hybrid format.

As computing continues to transform global industries and society, the future is emerging from the powerful convergence of Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Blockchain, IoT, Robotics, and Sustainable Systems. ICICSC 2027 unites leading researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, and innovators from across the globe to present cutting-edge breakthroughs and debate emerging directions.

Focusing on the transition from conventional computing toward intelligent, secure, resilient, and human-centric ecosystems, the conference welcomes original theoretical contributions, practical engineering solutions, industrial deployments, and interdisciplinary research addressing real-world challenges aligned with sustainable development goals.

Venue

Hosted on campus, connected to the world

Sharda University, Knowledge Park III, Greater Noida — the only multi-discipline campus in the Delhi NCR, spread over 63 acres.

The conference occupies the auditorium and seminar halls of the Sharda School of Computing Science & Engineering. Guest-house rooms and partner hotels at negotiated rates are available, and an airport shuttle runs on all conference days.
