Conference programme

A provisional day-by-day schedule for 27–29 May 2027, and what presenters need to prepare.

Provisional programme

The structure below is provisional and is published to help delegates plan travel and accommodation. The detailed programme, with paper titles, authors and session chairs, is published on 15 May 2027 and circulated by email to all registered delegates.
